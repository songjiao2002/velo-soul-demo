# Korea landmark map

Verified 2026-10-20. 20 curated map points in eight regional groups; includes the three pre-existing Seoul places. Coordinates are factual display points, not surveyed entrances, routes or cycling permissions. See public/landmark-sources.html for every coordinate and source. Wikimedia coordinates may be approximate. No copied descriptions or images.

Custom SVG icons distinguish palaces, temples/shrines, fortresses, villages, towers, mountains, coasts, parks and cafes. Labels, list, region controls, details and errors support Korean, English and Chinese. Projected nearby points group into counted buttons; each point remains reachable in the expandable list. Select a region or explore Korea to fit bounds. Explicit exploration does not get overridden by GPS updates; My location resumes centering.

Existing SEOUL_PLACES, weather, microphone, audio route, QR files and trigger thresholds are unchanged. New visual pins do not claim automatic spoken triggers. Browsing landmarks needs no location permission; GPS remains separately controlled.

Validation: dataset constraints/source links, clustering conservation, DOM region/detail/language/centering/cleanup behavior and existing application tests. Physical Samsung and map tile rendering require device checks; DOM tests do not simulate WebGL.

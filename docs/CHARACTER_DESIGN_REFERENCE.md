# Cel-shaded character design reference

The sole character reference is the user's original character board, retained
at public/assets/characters-original-20261025.jpg without changing its bytes.

- Mori: cream/orange cat, triangular pink ears, tan explorer helmet, oval
  goggles, orange backpack, striped tail and orange bicycle.
- Popo: round ivory hamster, pink ears, yellow helmet and bag, bread basket
  with a paw emblem, yellow bicycle and a handheld loaf.
- Lulu: white panda with black ears/eye patches/limbs, pale riding helmet,
  green backpack and bicycle; helmet removed for the sleeping pose.

Use rounded silhouettes, large heads and short bodies, fine warm outlines,
limited soft shadows and flat pastel fills. Keep the same character in the
cover, camera, chat and journal. The latest direction permits real 3D rotation
with an illustrated appearance: two discrete lighting bands, fine outlines,
orthographic projection, no glossy surfaces. The large companion uses articulated
geometry; small avatars and unsupported browsers keep the layered SVG version.
No portrait image is wrapped around a model. Lulu has a rounder face, charcoal
patches, subtle cheeks and a small curved smile. Keep the pale helmet's crown
rounded and raised, with a thin fitted brim and both ears visible. Its seam follows
the shell; the hat moves with the head. The rest pose rests the head on front paws,
with closed eyelids, tucked back paws, a green backpack and subtle breathing.
Maintain existing QR IDs
even when display names differ from those IDs.

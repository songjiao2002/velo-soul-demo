Privacy & permissions

VELO SOUL research prototype · Updated 25 October 2026

What you choose to share

  • Camera: scans bicycle QR codes on your device. Camera frames go to Alibaba Cloud Qwen only when AI observation or scene sharing is enabled. Camera frames are not retained by the VELO SOUL server.
  • Microphone: conversation audio goes through our server to Qwen for speech recognition. The app does not keep audio recordings. Chat text and selected scene/place/weather context also go to Qwen.
  • Paired headphones (Android 12+ app): Nearby devices permission allows access to an already paired Bluetooth headset during voice use. We do not scan for devices, pair them, or upload device identifiers. Audio output remains controlled by your phone.
  • Location: precise GPS trails stay on your device. An approximate 2 km grid is sent to our server and Open-Meteo for weather. Map tiles from OpenFreeMap disclose the viewed region and your network IP to that provider.
  • Journeys, photo previews and character settings are stored on this device. Native captures are stored in app-private storage; use the save/share action to export. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local records. Device backup policies may apply.
  • Bicycle memories are shared only after your explicit confirmation. Shared memories can be read by other riders, remain for up to 30 days, and can be deleted from the originating installation using its locally stored deletion token. Avoid personal information in shared notes.

While the app is open

Camera, voice and GPS are foreground features. They pause when the app is hidden. We do not request background recording or background location. No advertising SDK or tracking identifier is included.

Permissions and controls

Permissions are requested when you start the relevant feature. You can decline and still browse the companions. Change permissions in your phone’s system settings. You can remove journeys in Journeys and delete shared bicycle memories in the memory panel.

Service providers

AI: Alibaba Cloud Qwen. Hosting: Railway. Weather: Open-Meteo. Map tiles: OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap. Online conversation, weather and map tiles require a network connection. Each provider has its own processing and retention terms.
